## 🎙️ Recording Tips for the Camera

1. **Eye Level**: Keep your camera or smartphone lens right at eye level (avoid looking down at a laptop screen).
2. **First Sentence Energy**: Hit the first word ("What happens...") with strong vocal inflection. It hooks judges within the first 3 seconds.
3. **Natural Pauses**: Take a slight half-second breath between the Problem, Solution, and Innovation sections.
4. **Key Words to Emphasize**:
   - *"total amnesia"*
   - *"Code Nappers"*
   - *"Clauset"*
   - *"100% offline"*
   - *"Residency Shield"*
   - *"zero data loss"*
